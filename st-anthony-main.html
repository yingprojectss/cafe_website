ST. ANTHONY MAIN | RIVERPLACE

ABOUT

This location is in the Riverplace buiding at Main Exposition Hall on historic St.Anthony Main.

At this location we offer Cardigan Donuts M-Sat and Honey + Rye baked goods everyday. We also offer grab-and-go FRGMNT Fares.

This is a dog-friendly building.

Parking is available on the street or in the Riverplace Parking Ramp on 2nd St SE.

M-F 6:30am-6pm + Sat-Sun 7am-6pm
